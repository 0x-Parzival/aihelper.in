AI phone agent FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How much does AI inbound call handling cost?

The inbound call handling subscription is $200 USD. Contact us to confirm billing interval, included call volume, and telephony charges.

How much does inbound and outbound AI calling cost?

The subscription for inbound call answering plus outbound calls is $500 USD. Contact us to confirm billing interval, call volume, and telephony charges.

Can the AI phone agent make calls on its own?

No. An authorized person chooses the contact and starts each outbound call.

What does the AI phone agent do during a call?

It follows your business instructions, talks with the caller, and provides a transcript and summary for your team to review.

Can I use AI Helper in my country?

Phone service availability and calling costs depend on the destination country and telephony provider. Contact us to confirm support and pricing for your country.

Can callers tell they are speaking with AI?

Default greetings identify the assistant as AI. Keep that disclosure in any custom greeting.
